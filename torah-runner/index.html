Torah Runner

Run through the stories of the Torah: Noah's ark, Egypt, the Red Sea, Mount Sinai, the walls of Jericho and Jerusalem, then on to New York. Catch the golden letters to write words of the Torah on your scroll. Find all Ten Commandments, write at least 26 words, and reach the end of the road to face the final boss.

No runs yet. The road starts at Mount Ararat.

Tip: turn your phone sideways for a wider view.

How to play

Keyboard

  • ←→ change lane
  • ↑ or Space jump
  • ↓ slide, or drop fast in the air
  • P pause · M sound

Touch

  • Swipe left or right to change lane
  • Swipe up or tap to jump
  • Swipe down to slide

Catch

  • א Golden letters spell the word at the top
  • Manna for points
  • A heart gives back a life
  • ⟋ Run up a ramp to launch over a wall

Power-ups

  • Star of David shield blocks one crash
  • Jet pack flies you over everything
  • Magnet pulls manna in
  • Double manna
  • Shofar blasts the road clear
